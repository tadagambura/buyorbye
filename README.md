# BuyOrBye

**Buy less, buy better**

> #13 of 33 · Wave 2: Recurring keepers · Used: Weekly · Very easy · Revenue potential: Medium · Money & Work

## What it is

A purchase-decision companion, not just a calculator. Before you buy: compare unit prices, work out cost-per-use, check monthly vs annual. Tempted? Put it on a cool-off wishlist and BuyOrBye checks back in 7 or 30 days. Already bought it? Log each use and watch the cost-per-use fall, so you can see what was really worth it.

## Why it's in the portfolio

The calculators bring in free search traffic (people search 'price per kg calculator' and 'cost per wear' all the time). The cool-off wishlist and cost-per-use tracking turn a one-off tool into something people come back to, and the 'money saved by not buying' total makes great social content that points to StackUp.

## Who it's for

Shoppers comparing pack sizes, people trying to stop impulse buying, anyone deciding whether a big purchase or subscription is worth it.

**The problem:** Impulse buys and confusing prices. People rarely know whether something was worth it, and unit prices and multi-buy deals are hard to compare in the aisle.

## Version one

- Calculators: price-per-unit with mixed units, cost-per-use, monthly vs annual break-even, discounts and multi-buys
- Cool-off wishlist: add an item, pick 7 or 30 days, get asked 'still want it?'
- 'Saved by not buying' running total for the things you skipped
- Cost-per-use tracker: log uses of things you bought (gym, coat, coffee machine) and watch the cost per use drop
- Shareable cards: 'The 1 kg bag is 23% cheaper', 'I saved £340 by waiting'

**Example:** A user wants £120 trainers, adds them to a 30-day cool-off, says 'no' when asked a month later, and £120 goes into their saved total. They also log gym visits and watch the membership drop from £40 to £4.44 a visit.

## Later

- Purchase history and a yearly 'was it worth it' recap
- Deal and alternative links (affiliate), clearly labelled
- Home-screen widget for the cool-off list

## How it makes money

| Model | Detail |
|---|---|
| Free | All calculators and a small wishlist, with ads |
| Premium | £1.99/month or £9.99 lifetime: unlimited wishlist and tracked items, history, recap, no ads |
| Ads | AdSense on the web calculators, AdMob in the app |
| Affiliate | Deal and alternative links, clearly labelled |

## Animation and tone

**Playful.** Celebrations, confetti, cute characters, satisfying sounds and mild comic reactions. Nothing tragic.

- A skipped item waves goodbye ('bye!') and the saved-money jar fills


## AI

**None.** Not needed. Plain code does everything this app does.

## Watch-outs

A calculator on its own is a weak business; the wishlist and tracking are what make this an app. Affiliate links must never bias the 'is it worth it' answer.
